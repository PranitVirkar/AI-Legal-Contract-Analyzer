FREELANCE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is between MarketPro Inc. ("Client") and Arjun Patil ("Freelancer"), effective April 1, 2026.

1. Services
The Freelancer shall develop a mobile application according to the requirements provided by the Client.

2. Payment
The Client shall pay a total fee of INR 2,00,000. Fifty percent shall be paid before development begins and fifty percent upon final delivery.

3. Intellectual Property
Upon receipt of full payment, ownership of custom source code created specifically for the Client shall transfer to the Client.

4. Delay
The Freelancer shall inform the Client promptly if circumstances are likely to delay delivery.

5. Confidentiality
The Freelancer must keep Client information confidential.

6. Termination
The Client may terminate the agreement if the Freelancer fails to deliver the project after a written 15-day cure period.
