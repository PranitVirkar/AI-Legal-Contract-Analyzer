HIGH-RISK BUSINESS AGREEMENT

This Agreement is between Alpha Trading Pvt. Ltd. ("Company") and Beta Partners ("Partner"), effective August 1, 2026.

1. Services
The Partner shall provide business development and consulting services.

2. Payment
The Company shall pay INR 3,00,000 per month.

3. Penalty
If the Partner fails to meet any performance target, the Company may impose a penalty of INR 2,00,000 for each missed target without further notice.

4. Indemnity
The Partner shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company from all claims, losses, damages, expenses, and liabilities arising directly or indirectly from the Partner's activities.

5. Non-Compete
The Partner shall not provide similar services to any competing business for two years after termination.

6. Arbitration
Any dispute shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Mumbai, Maharashtra.

7. Automatic Renewal
This agreement shall automatically renew for one-year periods unless either party provides 90 days written notice.

8. Termination
The Company may terminate this agreement immediately and without compensation if it determines that the Partner has failed to meet performance expectations.
