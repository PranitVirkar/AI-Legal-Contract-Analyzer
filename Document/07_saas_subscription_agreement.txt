SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This SaaS Subscription Agreement is between CloudDesk Technologies ("Provider") and Orion Consulting ("Customer"), effective July 1, 2026.

1. Service
The Provider shall provide access to its cloud-based project management platform.

2. Subscription Fee
The Customer shall pay INR 20,000 per month for the subscription.

3. Automatic Renewal
The subscription shall automatically renew for additional one-month periods unless either party provides written cancellation notice at least 15 days before renewal.

4. Data
The Provider shall implement reasonable security measures to protect Customer data.

5. Service Availability
The Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain service availability.

6. Limitation of Liability
The Provider's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the subscription fees paid by the Customer during the previous six months.

7. Termination
Either party may terminate the subscription for material breach if the breach is not corrected within 15 days after written notice.
