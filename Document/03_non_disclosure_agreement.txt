NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is between Nova Analytics Pvt. Ltd. ("Disclosing Party") and Priya Mehta ("Receiving Party"), effective March 5, 2026.

1. Confidential Information
Confidential Information includes business plans, customer records, source code, algorithms, financial information, and technical documentation.

2. Obligations
The Receiving Party shall not disclose or use Confidential Information except for the purpose authorized by the Disclosing Party.

3. Security
The Receiving Party must take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access to Confidential Information.

4. Exceptions
Information that becomes publicly available without breach of this agreement shall not be considered Confidential Information.

5. Duration
The confidentiality obligations shall continue for three years after termination.

6. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.
