VENDOR AGREEMENT

This Vendor Agreement is between FreshMart Retail Pvt. Ltd. ("Buyer") and GreenSupply Foods ("Vendor"), effective June 15, 2026.

1. Supply
The Vendor shall supply packaged food products according to purchase orders issued by the Buyer.

2. Quality
The Vendor must ensure that all products comply with applicable quality and safety requirements.

3. Payment
The Buyer shall pay approved invoices within 30 days of receipt.

4. Indemnity
The Vendor shall indemnify the Buyer against third-party claims arising from defective products supplied by the Vendor.

5. Liability
The Vendor shall be responsible for direct losses caused by defective products.

6. Termination
The Buyer may terminate this agreement immediately if the Vendor repeatedly supplies products that fail quality requirements.

7. Confidentiality
The Vendor must keep pricing, purchase volumes, and business information confidential.
