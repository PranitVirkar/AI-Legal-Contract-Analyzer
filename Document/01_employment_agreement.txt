EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is between Apex Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Company") and Rahul Sharma ("Employee"), effective January 1, 2026.

1. Position and Duties
The Employee shall work as a Software Engineer and shall perform duties assigned by the Company.

2. Compensation
The Company shall pay the Employee a monthly salary of INR 75,000. Salary shall be paid on the last working day of each month.

3. Confidentiality
The Employee must keep confidential all proprietary information, source code, customer information, and business information of the Company.

4. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 30 days written notice. The Company may terminate the agreement immediately for serious misconduct or material breach.

5. Intellectual Property
All software and documentation created by the Employee in the course of employment shall belong to the Company.

6. Governing Law
This agreement shall be governed by the laws applicable in Maharashtra, India.
