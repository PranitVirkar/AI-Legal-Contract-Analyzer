SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Software Services Agreement is between Bright Retail Ltd. ("Client") and CodeCraft Solutions ("Service Provider"), effective February 10, 2026.

1. Services
The Service Provider shall develop and maintain a web application for the Client.

2. Payment
The Client shall pay INR 4,50,000 for development services. An advance payment of INR 1,50,000 is due upon signing. The remaining amount shall be paid after delivery.

3. Delivery
The Service Provider shall deliver the first production version within 90 days.

4. Liability
The Service Provider shall be responsible for direct damages caused by intentional misconduct or gross negligence.

5. Confidentiality
Both parties must protect confidential technical and business information.

6. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with 15 days written notice if the other party materially breaches the agreement.
