RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is between Sunil Deshmukh ("Landlord") and Amit Joshi ("Tenant"), effective May 1, 2026.

1. Property
The Landlord agrees to rent the residential apartment located in Pune, Maharashtra, to the Tenant.

2. Rent
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of INR 25,000 on or before the fifth day of each month.

3. Security Deposit
The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of INR 75,000.

4. Maintenance
The Tenant shall maintain the premises in reasonable condition and shall be responsible for damage caused by negligence.

5. Late Payment
A late payment penalty of INR 500 shall apply for each month in which rent is not paid by the due date.

6. Termination
Either party may terminate the agreement by providing 60 days written notice.

7. Renewal
The agreement may be renewed by mutual written consent.
